What is plastic surgery?
Plastic surgery is a surgical specialty that improves both appearance and function. It includes cosmetic procedures that enhance your features, and reconstructive procedures that repair parts of the body affected by burns, trauma, disease or congenital conditions.
Am I a good candidate?
Good candidates are in stable general health, are non-smokers (or willing to stop before surgery), and have realistic expectations. Suitability differs for every procedure, so a personal consultation is the best way to know.
What happens at my first consultation?
We'll discuss your concerns and goals, review your medical history, examine the area of concern and explain your options — including expected results, risks, recovery time and costs. Virtual consultations are also available.
How long is the recovery?
Recovery depends on the procedure. Non-surgical treatments often need little to no downtime, while surgical procedures may need from a few days to a few weeks. You'll receive detailed post-operative instructions and follow-up care.
How much does treatment cost?
Costs vary with the procedure, its complexity and the hospital facilities required. You'll receive a clear, itemised estimate after your consultation — with no hidden surprises.
Is it covered by insurance?
Medically necessary reconstructive procedures are often covered by insurance, while purely cosmetic procedures usually are not. Our team will help you understand your coverage and paperwork.
